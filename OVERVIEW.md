An end-to-end LLM fine-tuning pipeline for e-commerce customer support intent classification. Compares three approaches — zero-shot prompting, few-shot prompting, and LoRA fine-tuning — across accuracy, latency, and cost metrics. Fine-tuning Phi-3.5-mini with QLoRA on Apple Silicon achieved 99.4% accuracy and 3× faster inference compared to prompting baselines. Results are tracked in MLflow and exposed through a FastAPI service and an interactive Streamlit benchmark dashboard.
